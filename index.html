Four programs, one continuous path

A child can begin at four months old and graduate from the same community eighteen years later.

  • 0 to 3

    Nido

    The nest. First steps, first words, and Spanish from the very beginning.

  • 3 to 6

    Casa

    Practical life, sensorial discovery, and phonics in both Spanish and English.

  • 7 to 11

    Action

    Studies move into English while Spanish continues alongside.

  • 12 to 18

    Hilltop

    Adolescent work, real responsibility, and the road to graduation.

We help children become more

  • Confident

    Practical life activities develop organization, focus, and confidence.

  • Creative

    Sensorial work engages every sense through open exploration.

  • Problem solvers

    Mathematics arrives as challenges, games, and materials children enjoy.

  • Bilingual

    Phonics in both languages. Spanish from ages 0 to 6, English from 7 to 18.

  • Emotionally intelligent

    Peace education builds conflict resolution and resilience.

  • Life-long learners

    Geography, history, zoology, botany, dance, music, and art.

We nurture life skills and independent learning in a language immersion environment of joy, connection, and truth.

School is a place families come to

Field trips and nature walks

Children visit museums, theaters, and parks. The field trip calendar goes out in advance, so parents who can join us are welcome to come along.

Celebrations

We cut up our own vegetables for a Thanksgiving feast, make Christmas cookies, plant seeds in spring, and celebrate graduation. Unit studies usually end with a celebration, and birthday parties are open to friends.
