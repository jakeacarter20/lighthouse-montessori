How school works

  • Ages 0 to 5

    Attend in person at our Montessori House of the Children in Provo.

  • Ages 6 to 18

    Participate online, plus once a week in person in apprenticeship settings around the community.

Four programs, one continuous path

A child can begin at four months old and graduate from the same community eighteen years later.

  • 0 to 3

    Nido

    In person, in a carefully prepared Montessori environment that supports language development, movement, independence, and concentration.

  • 3 to 6

    Casa

    In person. Literacy, mathematics, practical life, sensorial exploration, cultural studies, grace and courtesy, and independence.

  • 7 to 11

    Action

    A Montessori hybrid program: project-based learning, leadership opportunities, outdoor experiences, meaningful work, and academic study.

  • 12 to 18

    Hilltop

    A hybrid online and in-person program: literature, writing, history, philosophy, mathematics, science, leadership, and apprenticeships.

We help children become more:

  • Confident

    Practical life activities develop organization, focus, and confidence.

  • Creative

    Sensorial works engage and fine-tune the senses, as children explore and problem solve.

  • Problem solvers

    Math challenges, games and materials which are understandable and enjoyable.

  • Good bilingual communicators

    Spoken, sung, written, read, language in Spanish (ages 0-6) and English (ages 7-18) used to connect and belong in our loving and fun environment. You will be surprised how much they learn and understand! As children are ready they learn letter formation, spelling and phonics in English and Spanish.

  • Emotionally intelligent

    Peace education helps children learn to contribute, set and respect boundaries, handle conflict wisely, understand others, collaborate, be resilient, strong and seek after peace.

  • Life-long learners

    Cultural experiences in geography, history, zoology, botany, dance, music and art feed wonder and curiosity. We put the world at the child’s fingertips.

About us

More about us

We nurture life skills and independent learning in a language immersion environment of joy, connection, and truth.

Field trips

We organize field trips and nature walks in which children visit museums, theaters and parks. You can expect the field trip calendar in advance so that if possible you can come and enjoy the fun with us!

Celebrations and community

Celebrations are a big part of what we do! We like to cut up our own veggies for a Thanksgiving feast, make Christmas cookies, plant seeds in spring and celebrate graduation. We end our unit studies frequently with celebrations. We like to invite friends to birthday parties and enhance a sense of community among our parents.

Thinking about joining us?

  • Admissions

    Five steps: enquiry, a school visit, application, placement review, then enrolment. Families are invited to visit, observe the environment, and meet school leadership.

  • Tuition

    Monthly tuition set by your child's age on September 1. Lighthouse is a licensed childcare program, and qualifying families may be eligible for assistance through Utah Workforce Services.

  • Our guides

    We call our teachers guides, because they are more than teachers. Montessori-trained, with backgrounds from special education and language immersion to classical literature and storytelling.
